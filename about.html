About the XORdemo

This demo is designed to complement Google's TensorFlow Playgound by visually illustrating the steps of the backpropagation learning algorithm. Focusing on the XOR (exclusive-or) problem, it shows how input values move through the network, how neuron outputs and output error are computed, how the error is propagated backward to the hidden units, and how weight adjustments (ΔW) are calculated and applied.

By stepping through the learning process, users can see how repeated weight updates gradually change the network's behavior as it learns the XOR problem.

Note that there are two solutions to the XOR problem:

  • The symmetric solution: (x1 AND NOT x2) OR (x2 AND NOT x1)
  • The asymmetric solution: (x1 OR x2) AND NOT (x1 AND x2)
Which solution the network ends up with depends on the initial weights.
